# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

Mindful Wallet

## Short description

Track spending, plan budgets, grow savings, and see your full money picture.

## Full description

Mindful Wallet helps you keep everyday finances clear and organized, without connecting
your bank accounts.

- Track expenses, income, and transfers across accounts.
- See your total balance in your chosen currency, with daily reference exchange rates.
- Set monthly budgets and savings goals.
- Explore spending patterns and forecasts, then revisit the month in a finance recap.
- Opt in to a gentle weekly reminder to review your finances.
- Import an M-PESA confirmation by sharing or pasting it, then review it before saving.
- Add transactions quickly from home-screen widgets.
- Protect app access with device authentication, including fingerprint on supported phones.
- Export statements and create a backup file when you choose.
- Learn the basics from a skippable onboarding tour.

Your transaction records stay on your device. Exchange-rate requests use currency codes,
not your account details or transaction history. There is no bank connection or account
sign-up. Optional receipt suggestions and monthly reflections use on-device AI on supported
phones.

Mindful Wallet is a personal tracking tool, not a bank or financial adviser.

## Listing assets to prepare

- App icon: 512 x 512 PNG.
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 PNG or JPEG.
- Phone screenshots captured from the release build, with no fabricated account data that
  could be mistaken for a real customer's finances.
- Tablet screenshots if the tablet experience will be promoted.
- Store contact email and the public privacy-policy URL.
